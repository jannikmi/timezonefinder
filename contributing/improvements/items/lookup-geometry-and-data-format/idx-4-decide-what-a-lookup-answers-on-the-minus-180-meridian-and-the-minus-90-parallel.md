# IDX-4 — decide what a lookup answers on the −180° meridian and the −90° parallel

- **What it is:** no polygon owns a point exactly on the −180° meridian or the −90° parallel, because the kernel gives a boundary point only to a polygon whose interior lies immediately west or south of it, and nothing lies there; [IDX-3](idx-3-pin-the-point-in-polygon-boundary-rule-with-a-test.md) pins the rule. So both indexes answer such a point with a cell's untested last zone, and the answer depends on the index: under the chosen grid tree 146 of 200 sampled points at exactly −180° change, mostly from `Etc/GMT-12` to `Etc/GMT+12` and three Chukotka points from `Asia/Anadyr` to the ocean, while none of 200 at −90° do ([aligned-index decisions](../../decisions/longitude-latitude-aligned-index-decisions.md)). The +180° meridian and the +90° parallel have no such gap: the polygon west of or south of them owns every point there.
- **The options to price:** keep that fallback, so answers follow the index; look −180° up as +180°, so both spellings of one meridian agree and land keeps its land zone; and for −90°, keep the fallback or answer the zone owning the point one unit north. Each moves answers.
- **The work:** measure each option against today's H3 answers and against the tree (`GridTree1` in `prototypes/latlng_index_bench.py`) — through `timezone_at`, `timezones_at`, `certain_timezone_at` and `TimezoneFinderL` — and record the briefed question here. The maintainer decides before [IDX-6](idx-6-replace-the-h3-shortcut-index-with-the-grid-tree.md)'s reader lands. An answer the index does not decide, the +180° spelling or the point one unit north, can ship on H3 ahead of IDX-6; keeping the fallback ships with IDX-6.
- **Documentation it settles:** the `certain_timezone_at()` note in `docs/1_usage.rst` and the exception paragraph near the top of `docs/result_stability.rst`. The note explains the −90° answer as polygon vertices meeting there, and both pages treat ±90° and ±180° alike, though every point on +90° and +180° is owned.
- **Size:** S to measure and brief; the implementation depends on the answer.
- **Status:** open — measure, then brief the question; IDX-6 waits on the answer.
