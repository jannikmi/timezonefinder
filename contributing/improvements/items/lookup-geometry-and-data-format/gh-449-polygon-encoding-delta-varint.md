- **Tracks:** issue #449, which carries the original measurements, the three transforms and the two candidate encodings. The issue is left as it stands and nothing reads it any more; this entry says why.
- **Superseded 2026-08-30 by the latitude block index - which shipped the same day, so its item file is gone - and by [FMT-2](fmt-2-a-frame-of-reference-payload-for-the-boundary-coordinates.md).** Steps 1 (AoS → SoA) and 2 (a format version constant) shipped long ago. What remained was a choice between two encodings, and measurement refused both — but the *goal* survived intact and moved to two items that reach it without the defect, so this is superseded rather than rejected outright.
- **What killed it, and this is the part worth keeping.** Both candidates — delta+zigzag+LEB128, and the int16-delta-with-escape variant the issue called "the variant actually worth prototyping" — are **sequential-decode** formats. LEB128 because element boundaries are only discoverable by scanning; delta because of the prefix-sum dependency. Each therefore puts an O(N) serial pass in front of the O(N) ray cast, and that ray cast is precisely where the package's worst latency already lives: measured on the clang path, `on_land` runs p50 1.00 µs against p99 39.71 µs, with query time correlating 0.92 with vertices tested and only 0.76 with candidate *count*. The issue's own decode figures — ~8.5 ms for the largest polygon under varint, ~828 µs under the best-case `cumsum` — sit against a ~20-55 µs point-in-polygon test. Even a perfectly fused C decoder at ~4.5 bytes/vertex is a 6-10x multiplier on the tail, which nothing in the ranking rules can absorb.
- **The size argument was never the problem and is not what was refused.** The numbers reproduce exactly: 35,320,271 B at 10⁻⁷ and 29,043,454 B at 10⁻⁶ against 63,402,504 B today. FMT-2 reaches 32,696,514 B at 10⁻⁶ *without* the sequential decode, which is why the compression goal is carried forward and only the encoding family is discarded.
- **Do not re-propose a sequential-decode polygon encoding on its size numbers.** The [geometry decisions](../../decisions/geometry-data-format-and-validation-decisions.md) carry the refusal in the permanent record, because a size table for one of these formats is exactly what would re-propose it.
- **Status:** withdrawn — superseded by the latitude block index and FMT-2, which reach the same size goal without putting a serial decode on the candidate loop.
- **Last touched:** 2026-08-30 — withdrawn after the tail was measured and the block-structured alternative priced and verified in `prototypes/polygon_block_encoding.py`.
