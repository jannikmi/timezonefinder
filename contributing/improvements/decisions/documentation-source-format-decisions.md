# Documentation source format decisions

Do not re-propose these settled or refused options without new evidence.

- **`docs/` leaves Sphinx for MkDocs; `README` and `CHANGELOG` become Markdown alongside it.** Decided 2026-09-06 by the maintainer, who asked to be rid of reStructuredText. The whole documentation tree becomes Markdown: `docs/` under MkDocs with Material and `mkdocstrings` ([DOC-9](../items/data-pipeline-and-developer-tooling/doc-9-migrate-the-documentation-from-sphinx-to-mkdocs.md)), the README ([DOC-7](../items/data-pipeline-and-developer-tooling/doc-7-move-the-readme-to-markdown-with-semantic-line-breaks.md)) and the changelog with its fragments ([DOC-8](../items/data-pipeline-and-developer-tooling/doc-8-move-the-changelog-and-its-fragments-to-markdown.md)) independently of it, since neither is Sphinx-built.
- **The point is that MkDocs' format is CommonMark, not directives in Markdown.** That is the whole difference from the MyST option refused below: MkDocs writes an admonition as `!!! note`, a table as a pipe table and code as a fence, so the markup counted below stops existing rather than changing spelling. Measured 2026-09-05/06, the Sphinx-specific surface is also smaller than the page count suggests — **14 autodoc directives, all on one 60-line page**, plus `viewcode`, `sphinx_rtd_theme` and a version read in `conf.py`; `intersphinx` is already commented out. No CI job builds the documentation, so nothing is rewired: `make docs` is manual and Read the Docs builds on push.
- **Two of the three arguments that refused Markdown in September reverse under a different generator, which is why the earlier decision did not survive contact with the question.** GitHub renders pipe tables and fences, so `docs/` sources become *more* readable in the repository rather than less — the opposite of what MyST does to them. And `mkdocs --strict` fails the build on a broken cross-reference, which `rstcheck` never checked at all: the [documentation maintenance rules](../../development/documentation-maintenance-rules.md) record that a renamed heading silently breaks every README anchor pointing at it today, so link validation is a net gain rather than the loss the `rstcheck` argument assumed.
- **A big-bang migration is planned and reviewed before it is implemented, and its conversion must be lossless.** Decided 2026-09-06 by the maintainer, alongside the generator choice and as a condition on it. The reason is specific to the shape of this change rather than general caution: a 5,332-line wholesale rewrite cannot be reviewed for *omissions*, because every hunk is new on both sides and a dropped paragraph reads exactly like a converted one. [DOC-10](../items/data-pipeline-and-developer-tooling/doc-10-plan-the-mkdocs-migration-for-maintainer-review.md) produces the plan and defines the losslessness contract — what is preserved, what is deliberately given up, and a runnable check for each — and [DOC-9](../items/data-pipeline-and-developer-tooling/doc-9-migrate-the-documentation-from-sphinx-to-mkdocs.md) is blocked until that plan is approved. **A plan that finds the contract unaffordable reopens the generator choice**, which is why the refused options above are kept rather than deleted.
- **What it costs, accepted with the decision.** It is **big-bang**: MkDocs cannot read reStructuredText at all, so all 5,332 lines of `docs/` convert in one reviewable change with no half-way state to retreat to — unlike MyST, which coexists with `.rst` indefinitely. The 2,123 generated lines carry the same emitter rewrite either way: `render_rst_table`, `rst_title` and `compute_column_widths` in `scripts/reporting.py`, plus regenerating every committed measurement. Wide benchmark tables lose `:widths:` and multi-line cells unless those pages emit raw HTML tables. And the 310 `:param:`/`:return:` docstring field lines need `griffe`'s Sphinx style parser — supported, but the least-travelled of the three it offers.
- **PDF and ePub are given up, on an assumption rather than a measurement.** `readthedocs.yml` declares `formats: all` and MkDocs has no such builder. Nobody knows whether either is read; the maintainer chose to assume not, and this bullet is the record of that so a single complaint reopens the decision rather than being argued against a claim of evidence nobody has. **That complaint is new evidence** — it is the one thing that would put option A or C back on the table.
- **Refused: MyST, keeping Sphinx and respelling the markup in Markdown.** Recorded 2026-09-05 as the *accepted* position for `docs/` and **superseded 2026-09-06** when the generator question was put properly — switching generator had not been among the options, and it is the only route that removes directive syntax rather than respelling it. The measurement behind that refusal stands and is what still rules MyST out: counted across `docs/`, `README.rst` and `CHANGELOG.rst` on 2026-09-05, 42 `list-table`, 36 `code-block`, 28 `note`, 21 `image`, 14 autodoc directives, 4 `toctree`, 3 `include`, and in roles 73 `:doc:`, 25 `:ref:`, 18 `:sup:` and 11 `:meth:`/`:func:` — every one of which survives MyST as `` ```{list-table} `` and `` {doc}`…` ``. MyST pays most of the migration cost for markup that changes spelling, makes `docs/` render worse on GitHub, and loses `rstcheck` with nothing taking over. Its one genuine advantage over MkDocs is that it is incremental and abandonable; that did not outweigh the rest.
- **Refused: keep Sphinx and leave `docs/` in reStructuredText.** The status quo, and the position recorded on 2026-09-05 before the generator question was asked. It costs nothing and keeps PDF, ePub and `rstcheck` — and it means writing reStructuredText in `docs/` indefinitely, which is what the maintainer asked twice to stop doing. Reinstate it only via the PDF/ePub trigger above.
- **Refused: `pdoc`, and the JavaScript-toolchain generators.** `pdoc` replaces autodoc well and does nothing for the 15 hand-written narrative pages, so it is not an answer on its own. Quarto, Docusaurus and Hugo are all capable static generators of the wrong shape for a Python library — either a JavaScript toolchain to maintain or an academic publishing pipeline nothing here needs.
- **The mixed tree stops being a cost once this lands, and is one until then.** `.rst` and `.md` side by side is a tax on every author, human or agent, who has to know which syntax a file wants. It was accepted on 2026-09-05 as permanent; under this decision it is temporary, and it is the strongest reason not to leave DOC-9 half-done or indefinitely deferred.
