A lookup now reads ``lng=-180.0`` as ``lng=180.0``, the same meridian, and ``lat=-90.0`` as the point one stored unit (~1.1 cm) north of the South Pole, so a point on either line answers the same zone whichever way it is spelled. As spelled, no polygon owns such a point: the point-in-polygon test gives a point on a boundary to the polygon west of it, or south of it along an east-west edge, and nothing lies west of -180° or south of -90°. So ``certain_timezone_at()`` returned ``None`` on both lines, and on the -180° meridian ``timezone_at()``, ``timezones_at()`` and the batch lookups answered the last zone the shortcut index lists for the cell without testing it, which in places owned neither side of the meridian; they now answer what they answer at ``lng=180.0``. ``TimezoneFinderL`` answers do not change.
