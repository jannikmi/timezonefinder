Where two zones' polygons overlap, ``timezone_at()`` now answers the zone with the smaller spherical area - the one enclaved in the other - in every cell of the shortcut index, with the timezone identifier breaking an exact tie. Before, the pick in an overlap followed the zones' vertex counts within each index cell, a property of the index rather than of the zones that a data update could flip. ``timezones_at()`` still returns both zones, now with the smaller one first. Compared with ``timezonefinder-data`` 3.2026.4, eight overlaps change answer, each as a whole region: Kafia Kingi and Abyei from ``Africa/Khartoum`` to ``Africa/Juba``, the West Bank overlap from ``Asia/Jerusalem`` to ``Asia/Hebron``, the Southern Patagonian Ice Field from ``America/Punta_Arenas`` to ``America/Argentina/Rio_Gallegos``, the Croatia-Serbia Danube border from ``Europe/Belgrade`` to ``Europe/Zagreb``, the Bay of Piran from ``Europe/Zagreb`` to ``Europe/Ljubljana``, a strip of the Strait of Juan de Fuca from ``America/Los_Angeles`` to ``America/Vancouver``, and slivers of the Sint Maarten border from ``America/Marigot`` to ``America/Lower_Princes`` and of the Nova Scotia-New Brunswick border from ``America/Halifax`` to ``America/Moncton``. The other overlaps, among them ``Asia/Urumqi`` inside ``Asia/Shanghai`` and ``Asia/Tbilisi`` over ``Europe/Moscow``, keep their answer. ``TimezoneFinderL`` answers a multi-zone cell with the zone the full lookup tests last, which wherever the converter keeps this order is now the largest of the cell's zones, so its answer moves beyond the overlaps, mostly in coastal cells beside an ocean zone: at 499 of the 1,843,200 centres of the index's smallest cells, where ``TimezoneFinder`` moves at 75, all inside the overlaps above.
