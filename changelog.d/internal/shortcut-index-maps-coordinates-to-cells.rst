The shortcut index now maps a coordinate to its cell itself (``ShortcutIndex.entry_at`` and ``entries_at``): the lookup methods, the benchmark fixture generator and the tests that resolve a point hand it a longitude and latitude instead of computing an H3 cell id of their own, and the shortcut-ordering calibration's stand-in index answers the same question, so replacing the index's cells needs no change to ``timezonefinder.py``.
