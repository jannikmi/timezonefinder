``TimezoneFinder`` looks up a boundary polygon's holes with a dictionary ``get`` returning a ``range``, instead of a generator whose body raises and catches a ``KeyError``. 1,225 of the 1,322 packaged boundary polygons own no hole, so the majority of point-in-polygon candidates used to build a generator, raise, catch and return in order to establish that there was nothing to check - now 88 ns per such candidate against 160. The private helper is renamed from ``_iter_hole_ids_of`` to ``_hole_ids_of``, since it no longer iterates.
