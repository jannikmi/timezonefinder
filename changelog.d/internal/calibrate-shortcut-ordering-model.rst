``make shortcut-calibration`` fits the shortcut-ordering costs from counted predicate stages, re-orders every ambiguous cell with the fitted and an area-only model, and A/B-times whole lookups on the cells that change; ``make shortcut-calibration-record`` keeps a reviewed run in ``benchmarks/shortcut_ordering_model.json``. The production coefficients stay: on the aligned grid tree the fitted order changes 3% of ambiguous cells with no resolvable speed-up, and the fit cannot separate the hole probe from the point-in-polygon dispatch it precedes, so it prices that dispatch at nearly nothing.
