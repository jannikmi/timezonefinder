The notes on ``certain_timezone_at()`` and ``timezones_at()`` and the certainty-probe paragraph of the alternatives page now say where no polygon contains a point: on the ``lng=-180.0`` meridian, at the exact South Pole, and in slivers the stored boundaries of neighbouring zones leave between them. A point on a border two zones share goes to one of them, and ``lng=180.0`` and ``lat=90.0`` are owned like any other edge.
