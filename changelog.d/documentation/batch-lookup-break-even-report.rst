New page `When batched lookups pay <https://timezonefinder.readthedocs.io/en/latest/benchmark_results_batch_break_even.html>`__: the batch API amortises validation, coordinate scaling and the shortcut table read over a whole batch, so a small batch is *slower* per point than a loop of scalar calls and a large one stops improving - the page measures where each of those happens, as a sweep of paired comparisons over a ladder of batch sizes. Both answers depend on the caller's coordinates as much as on their hardware, because points sharing an H3 cell are answered together, so ``scripts/measure_batch_break_even.py`` (``make batch-break-even``) takes ``--points your.csv`` and reports the same two numbers for your own workload.
