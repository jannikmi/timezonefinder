New page `Result stability and reproducibility <https://timezonefinder.readthedocs.io/en/latest/result_stability.html>`__: a lookup is a pure function of the coordinates and the installed data, and the three channels through which a ``timezonefinder-data`` update can still change an answer - boundary geometry, polygon precedence at shared borders, and shortcut composition and ordering. Candidate reordering cannot move a ``TimezoneFinder`` result, but it can change a ``TimezoneFinderL`` suggestion anywhere in a multi-zone cell even when the geometry is unchanged; pinning the data distribution is what freezes all three.
