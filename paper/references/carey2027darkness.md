> A crash was classified as occurring in darkness if the crash timestamp, localized to the crash's time zone using timezonefinder, occurred after civil dusk—the moment when the sun is 6 …

## Role of timezonefinder

Fatal pedestrian crashes (US data, crash coordinates plus timestamp) are localised to the crash's time zone and compared with civil dusk and dawn at that location. The result is the darkness indicator, the exposure variable of the study.

## Notes

- Like [`manchin2025collaboration`](manchin2025collaboration.md), the lookup feeds the study's key variable directly. A wrong zone near a US time-zone border (e.g. in split counties in Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee, Florida or the Dakotas) would shift a crash by an hour relative to dusk. This is a use where accuracy at borders matters.
