## Role of timezonefinder

Co-author affiliations are geocoded, and timezonefinder assigns each affiliation a time zone; the time-zone difference between collaborators is the paper's key explanatory variable for citation impact.

## Notes

- A use where timezonefinder output is not just preprocessing but defines the treatment variable of the study, so lookup errors at borders would propagate directly into the estimates.
